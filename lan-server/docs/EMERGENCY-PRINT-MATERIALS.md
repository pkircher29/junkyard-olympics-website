# Emergency public print materials

For total local Wi-Fi failure, switch to paper immediately. The reproducible packet in `artifacts/public-print-packet/` supersedes the older eight-page browser capture in `design/evidence/emergency-packet.pdf` for pre-event copying.

The packet includes:

- index and exact copy order;
- public signup sign;
- reusable signs for all eight official station routes;
- score/check-in sheets for Ladder Ball, Field Pong, Cornhole, KanJam, Lawn Darts, Bocce Ball, Volley Strike, and Washers;
- separate Junkyard Cannon Lane 1 and Lane 2 paper ledgers.

Horseshoes and Badminton are labeled casual only and intentionally have no station QR. Cannon is recorded on its two-lane ledger and intentionally has no field station QR.

Build and verification instructions are in [`../tools/print_packet/README.md`](../tools/print_packet/README.md). Generation is local only: do not deploy or physically print automatically. Use `MANIFEST.txt` as the copy handoff and checksum list.
